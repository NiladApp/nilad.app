Nilad

Privacy

Written plainly. The short version: your documents never leave your Mac.

What runs on your Mac

Everything. Text recognition, classification, field extraction and search all use Apple's on-device frameworks — the Vision framework and Foundation Models on the Neural Engine. There is no Nilad server. There couldn't be a data breach of your documents at our end, because nothing of yours is at our end.

What is stored, and where

Your library is a single package on your Mac containing the original files and what Nilad read from them. If you choose iCloud in onboarding, it syncs through your own iCloud account under Apple's terms — we never see it.

What touches the network

Nothing, from the app itself. Purchases and subscriptions go through the App Store — that's between your Apple account and Apple. The app has no analytics, no crash reporter that phones home, no accounts and no email list. The app's Settings include a network log; the honest expectation is that it stays empty.

This website

Static pages, no cookies, no trackers, self-hosted fonts.

How to leave

Settings → Library → Export everything. You get your original files plus a CSV of everything Nilad read, whether or not you're paying. Then delete the app. Done.
