Common questions

Which Macs does Nilad run on?
Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later. The on-device reading needs the Neural Engine, so Intel Macs can't be supported.
Nilad read something wrong.
Click any field to correct it — corrections teach Nilad how documents from that sender look. Low-confidence documents already wait for you in Needs review.
Where is my library stored?
One package on your Mac. Everything stays local unless you chose iCloud in onboarding. Settings → Library shows the location and exports everything with one click.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. If it lapses, your library stays readable and exportable — only new drops pause.
Refunds?
Through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you're stuck, email us and we'll point you the right way.
