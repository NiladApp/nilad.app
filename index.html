The problem

“IMG_2047.jpg” is not a filing system.

Three hundred files in Downloads. Photos of paperwork lost in your camera roll. A Finder search that finds nothing. You already have the documents — what you don't have is the ten seconds it takes to file each one properly, times three hundred.

How it works

Organise PDFs, receipts and bills.

Drop PDFs, images or text files into the library or onto the menu-bar icon. On Macs with a notch, drop them there too. Choose index-in-place if you want originals to stay where they are.

2
How it works

Read scanned documents with OCR.

Nilad reads text with on-device OCR. When Apple Intelligence is available, it suggests a document type and useful details. Review uncertain results and edit any field. Your corrections help guide future suggestions from the same sender.

How it works

Search document contents, not just filenames.

The dishwasher's warranty. The insurance policy. Anything from 2026. Even “agl over 200”. Search recognised text and reviewed details, then narrow the results by type, party or date. Save useful filters so you can find the same group again.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your Mac.

Nilad processes documents with Apple's on-device frameworks. It has no document server, no Nilad account and no analytics. Apple handles purchases and subscriptions. Prefer your files where they are?
